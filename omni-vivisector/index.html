AETERNA SECURE OPERATIVE MANUAL & REGULATIONS

SLA service levels, Safe Harbor codes of conduct, game theory bounty rules, and identity compliance guidelines.

1. Safe Harbor Policy & Allowed Behaviors (Безопасно Пристанище)

Trust and mutual security are built on ironclad rules that protect both the protocol’s architecture and the ethical researcher's rights. Under Aeterna’s Safe Harbor policy, sponsors agree never to take legal or hostile action against whitehats, provided they strictly abide by the rules.

🚫 STRICTLY PROHIBITED BEHAVIORS
  • Mainnet Scanning/Testing: Strictly prohibited. All scanning, testing, and exploitation must be carried out exclusively on local forks.
  • DDoS & Mass Traffic: Generating heavy traffic using automated tools or flood scanning is forbidden.
  • Social Engineering & Phishing: Targeting protocol employees, physical office entry, or extortion leads to an immediate, permanent ban.
  • Fabricating/Exaggerating Facts: Intentional inflation of severities or reporting theoretical flaws as critical.
🛡️ PROTECTED B2B PROTOCOL RULES
  • Anti-Stealth Fixing: Sponsors are strictly prohibited from secretly patching reported vulnerabilities to bypass reward payments.
  • No Out-of-Bounds Negotiations: Sponsors cannot negotiate directly with wardens for lower payouts outside the platform.
  • Known Issue Declarations: Sponsors cannot declare a finding as a "known issue" unless they provide a timestamped public audit or internal ticket dated before the submission.

2. Economic Model, Game Theory & Payouts (Икономически Модел)

To ensure the expected value (EV) of reporting a bug is always higher than malicious exploitation (taking legal risks and money laundering friction into account), B2B reward systems must follow dynamic, TVL-proportional game theory formulas rather than flat-rate rewards.

Proportional TVL Payout Formula Reward = min(max(0.10 * TVL_affected, Minimum_cap), Maximum_cap)
Our B2B programs incentivize researchers at early stages (with a high Minimum Cap floor like $100,000) and protect protocol solvency at high liquidity pools (with a Maximum Cap ceiling like $1,000,000).
⚡ Primacy of Impact (Примат на Въздействието)

If an attack path leads to a catastrophic loss of assets, the finding is classified as Critical and receives maximum reward, even if the specific vulnerable component was not explicitly outlined in the initial written scope. This protects whitehats against semantic loopholes.

⚖️ High-Severity Lock multiplier (DoS)

For high-severity vulnerabilities causing Denial of Service or temporary lockups of funds, rewards scale proportionally to the duration of the lock (e.g. doubling for every 5 blocks locked) up to a pre-defined hard cap.

3. Global KYC/AML & OFAC Compliance (Легитимност & Санкции)

Since our platform facilitates B2B transfers of large financial sums, strict adherence to client identification (KYC) and anti-money laundering (AML) laws is a binding legal duty.

  • Sanction Check: Under zero circumstances will rewards be disbursed to individuals residing in sanctioned jurisdictions or listed under US OFAC, European Union, or UN Security Council (UNSC) blacklists.
  • Verification Gate: Before releasing high-tier payouts (above $10,000 USD), researchers must undergo standard identity verification requiring a government-issued photo ID and address proof.
  • Secure Cryptographic Auditing: Payment allocations are Denominated in USD but paid out in stablecoins (USDC/USDT) directly to verified, non-custodial multi-signature wallets to guarantee decentralized accountability.

4. Technical Triage & PoC Requirements (Правила за Триаж)

To eliminate spam and ensure Aeterna’s triage engineers focus exclusively on real, verified, and executable bugs, all warden report submissions are filtered through a strict cryptographic triage adapter.

  • Description: A detailed written summary explaining the logical vulnerability root cause and impact vector is required.
  • Reproduction Steps: A step-by-step sequential guide describing how to replicate the exploit state.
  • Working Proof of Concept (PoC) Code: A complete, executable Solidity/Foundry test skeleton showing the actual exploit mechanism. Submissions without a valid PoC are automatically rejected by our triage adapter.

THE WALL OF LOGIC (FAQ)

Which programming languages are supported?

OMNI-VIVISECTOR has full out-of-the-box static compilation and taint mapping support for **Solidity** (.sol), **Rust** (.rs), **Python** (.py), and **TypeScript** (.ts). The engine automatically adapts its parsing grammar to match target AST structures.

What happens after the audit completes?

Once you export your signed Forensic Dossier, the RAM memory allocated to the isolated WASM hypervisor is instantly flushed with zero-values (Zeroization). OMNI-VIVISECTOR enforces a strict **Zero Retention Policy** - no cache files, temp variables, or logging buffers remain on your disk.

Is the report format compatible with major security portals?

Absolutely. The generated audit dossier is compiled directly into a stylized **Markdown / PDF structure** that perfectly matches the official submission formats required by leading platforms like **Immunefi** and **Code4rena**.
