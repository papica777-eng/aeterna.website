Which programming languages are supported?

OMNI-VIVISECTOR has full out-of-the-box static compilation and taint mapping support for **Solidity** (.sol), **Rust** (.rs), **Python** (.py), and **TypeScript** (.ts). The engine automatically adapts its parsing grammar to match target AST structures.

What happens after the audit completes?

Once you export your signed Forensic Dossier, the RAM memory allocated to the isolated WASM hypervisor is instantly flushed with zero-values (Zeroization). OMNI-VIVISECTOR enforces a strict **Zero Retention Policy** - no cache files, temp variables, or logging buffers remain on your disk.

Is the report format compatible with major security portals?

Absolutely. The generated audit dossier is compiled directly into a stylized **Markdown / PDF structure** that perfectly matches the official submission formats required by leading platforms like **Immunefi** and **Code4rena**.
