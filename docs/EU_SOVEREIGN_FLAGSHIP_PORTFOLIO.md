## 🛰️ Detailed Action Dossiers

### 1. AETERNA-SCW — Smart Cables Works (€20.0M)
* **Objective:** Deployment of the European Black Sea coastal quantum terminal in Pomorie (Bulgaria), integrating continuous-variable optical sensing and seismic/acoustic monitoring directly into high-throughput subsea telecommunications fiber.
* **Geopolitical Significance:** Safeguards EU critical connectivity against underwater physical sabotage, securing the southeastern corridor linking the Black Sea through the Aegean to Central Europe.
* **Consortium:** AETERNA Technologies (Coordinator, Terminal Operator), GRNET S.A. (Greece, National Optical Backbone), LMU München (Germany, Photonic Routing).

### 2. AETERNA-QSTN — Submarine Defense & Quantum Tactical Shield (€22.19M)
* **Objective:** Sovereign cyber-physical shield leveraging post-quantum lattice cryptography (ML-KEM-1024 / ML-DSA-87) and hardware-anchored zero-copy acoustic sensor nodes for naval command resilience.
* **Regulatory Alignment:** Regulation (EU) 2021/697 (EDF), NIS2 Directive (EU 2022/2555), Common Criteria EAL4+ equivalent hardware isolation.
* **Consortium:** AETERNA Technologies (Bulgaria, Coordinator), LMU München (Germany, Quantum Optics Division), EETT (Greece, National Telecommunications).

### 3. AETERNA-QKD — EuroHPC Supercomputing Backbone (€8.0M)
* **Objective:** Direct integration of high-bandwidth QKD transceivers into EuroHPC petascale/exascale supercomputers (including Barcelona Supercomputing Center's MareNostrum 5).
* **Technological Highlights:** Sub-shot-noise photonic squeezing, phase-noise cancellation, and FPGA-accelerated zero-entropy error reconciliation operating at gigabit-per-second key generation rates.
* **Consortium:** AETERNA Technologies (Coordinator), LMU München (Germany), Barcelona Supercomputing Center (Spain), GRNET (Greece).

### 4. AETERNA-VHT — Virtual Human Twin for Cancer & UNCAN.eu (€9.85M)
* **Objective:** Multi-scale in-silico biophysical modeling and longitudinal treatment simulation for oncology patients, fully integrated with the European UNCAN.eu research data platform.
* **Interoperability & Data Standards:** OMOP-CDM v5.4 Oncology Extension, HL7 FHIR Release 5, GA4GH Phenopackets v2, federated containerized GA4GH WES edge execution.
* **Clinical Protocol:** Prospective non-interventional cohort study across 5 longitudinal timepoints ($T_0 \to T_4$), with dedicated Months 1–6 Multi-Site IRB/IEC ethical review window.
* **Consortium:** AETERNA Technologies (Coordinator), Charité / IMO (Germany), NKI / Gustave Roussy (Netherlands/France), Nordic EHR Alliance (Sweden), LC InnoConsult (Hungary), European Cancer Patient Coalition (Belgium).

### 5. OMNI-VIVISECTOR — ERC Plus Frontier Science (€7.0M)
* **Objective:** Foundational formal verification engine combining Weisfeiler-Lehman (WL) AST graph embedding with 4-state non-classical Catuskoti logic to achieve $O(1)$ structural zero-day discovery in distributed computing architectures.
* **Empirical Validation:** Proven on high-throughput Tier-1 production codebases (Firedancer VM sandbox escape, Base Azul SpanBatch panic DoS).
* **Principal Investigator:** Dimitar Stavrev Prodromov | **Host Institution:** AETERNA Technologies.
