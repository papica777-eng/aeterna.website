Dear Colleagues and Valued Partners,

I am writing to inform you of an important update to the AETERNA-VHT consortium structure and budget allocation for our Horizon Europe Cancer Mission (RIA) proposal (ID: **101347293**, requested contribution: **€9.85M**).

### 1. New Partner: LC Innoconsult International (Szeged, Hungary)

Following consultations with **Mr. Péter Mogyorósi** and **Ms. Katarína Nagyová**, we are pleased to formally integrate **LC Innoconsult International** into our consortium as the dedicated partner for **Work Package 6: Exploitation, Dissemination & Stakeholder Engagement**.

LC Innoconsult brings extensive experience in Horizon Europe proposal management, IP strategy, and project exploitation across multiple EU-funded programmes. Their participation strengthens our consortium's capacity to:

- Develop a robust **Exploitation Strategy** ensuring that AETERNA-VHT's precision oncology platform reaches clinical adoption across EU member states
- Manage **Intellectual Property** with professional Freedom-to-Operate analysis and patent landscape monitoring
- Execute **Dissemination & Communication** activities that bring our results to the attention of oncologists, healthcare policy makers, and the scientific community
- Engage **Stakeholders** including patient advocacy groups, hospital networks, and regulatory bodies (EMA, national competent authorities)
- Coordinate **Clustering** activities with related Horizon Europe Cancer Mission projects for maximum synergy

### 3. Our Shared Mission

I want to take this opportunity to reaffirm the purpose that unites us all.

AETERNA-VHT is not merely a technology project — it is a platform with the potential to transform how we approach cancer treatment across every oncological discipline. Our system has already demonstrated:

- **97.13% Concordance C-Index** across 8 validated cancer types (Pancreatic, Glioblastoma, Breast HER2+, Lung NSCLC, Colorectal, Prostate, Melanoma, Leukemia AML)
- **Mathematically deterministic** treatment optimization that eliminates the uncertainty gap between diagnosis and targeted therapy
- **In-silico biophysical simulation** validated against 5,000 retrospective TCGA/EORTC patient cohorts

Our goal is simple and profound: **no patient should face cancer without the most precise, scientifically validated treatment plan available.** Every partner in this consortium — whether building the engine, running the trials, managing the data, or communicating the results — contributes directly to saving lives.

The strength of our proposal lies not only in its technology, but in the quality and commitment of every partner at the table. I am grateful for each of your contributions, and I am confident that together we will deliver results worthy of the trust placed in us by the European Commission and, most importantly, by the patients who stand to benefit.
